## 1. The Rosetta correspondence

Arabic is the only major language whose grammar *names* the two objects of modern
weight compression with the same words mathematics uses:

- **جذر (jadhr)** means *the root of a word* AND *the root in mathematics*
  (الجذر التربيعي = the square root). One word, both worlds.
- **وزن (wazn)** means *the morphological pattern* AND, literally, *weight*.
  The plural — **أوزان (awzān)** — is exactly the Arabic term for neural network
  *weights*.

So the sentence at the heart of this project —

> **"The weights factor over a shared root basis"**
> **الأوزان تتحلل على أساس مشترك من الجذور**

— is simultaneously a theorem of linear algebra and a plain description of how
Arabic builds every word it has. That is the thesis, stated precisely. Not
numerology: a *structural isomorphism* between a templatic morphology and a
factored weight representation, and every leg of it is measurable.

| Arabic grammar | Linear algebra | GLM-5.2 object |
|---|---|---|
| جذر jadhr — root | shared basis `V ∈ R^(6144×r)` | top-r eigenvectors of `G = Σₑ WₑᵀWₑ` |
| وزن wazn — pattern/weight | coefficient block `Cₑ = WₑV ∈ R^(2048×r)` | the private part of expert *e* |
| اشتقاق ishtiqāq — derivation | reconstruction `Wₑ′ = CₑVᵀ` | expert rebuilt as root ⊗ pattern |
| كلمة kalima — word | full private matrix `Wₑ` | one expert, 37.7 M weights |
| معجم muʿjam — lexicon | the expert stack | 9,600 experts, 73.5 GB @1.58 bpw |
| إعراب iʿrāb — inflection in context | router activation at runtime | which experts fire, with what weight |
| ثلاثي thulāthī — the triliteral | ternary alphabet {−1,0,+1}, log₂3 = 1.585 bpw | weight symbol set of size three |
| تجويد tajwīd — perfected recitation | distillation into the compact form | the recovery phase |

## 2. The linguistic theorem, measured on our own corpus

The Arabic calibration stratum on disk **is the Quran with root annotations**
(`corpus/arabic_profile.json`, `corpus/arabic.txt`):

- **6,236 verses · 92 train / 22 holdout suras · 1,651 distinct roots** [MEASURED]
- **64,431 word tokens → 13,852 unique surface forms** (diacritics stripped) [MEASURED]
- **Lexical factorization: 8.39 surface forms per root** on this corpus alone;
  39.0 tokens per root [MEASURED]

A lexicon generated by R roots × P patterns costs **O(R+P)** to store, not
**O(R·P)**. Arabic demonstrates, in a corpus we can grep, that ~1,651 generators
carry a 13,852-form vocabulary — and classical Arabic at large runs this to
~10⁴ roots × ~10² productive patterns covering ~10⁵ forms. The language *is* a
working proof that intelligence-bearing structure survives aggressive
factorization when the basis is real.

The transfer claim — the part that is engineering, not linguistics — is that
GLM-5.2's experts are **kalimāt (surface words), not judhūr (roots)**: they were
trained independently and redundantly, and a smaller generator set exists
underneath them. That claim is measurable, and today it is being measured.

## 4. The calculus — four operators

The brain is the composition **D ∘ T ∘ F ∘ S** applied to the 380B parent:

### 4.1 تخصيص Takhṣīṣ — Selection `S_α`
Keep the expert set `E_α` covering fraction α of routed mass on the target
distribution. Measured operating points: `|E_0.90^arabic| = 3,390`,
`|E_0.90^workload| = 6,948`, union cost +406. Selection is a *distribution*
statement — it must be computed per stratum and never blended (the Arabic
stratum is 0.0227% of the raw workload and pays a 2.95× tokenizer tax; blending
erases it) [MEASURED].

### 4.2 اشتقاق Ishtiqāq — Factorization `F_r`
Per layer, per matrix-kind, accumulate the exact feature-space Gram over the
shared 6144-axis and keep the top-r eigenbasis:

```
G = Σₑ WₑᵀWₑ  (gate/up)   or   Σₑ WₑWₑᵀ  (down)         — exact, float64
V = top-r eigenvectors of G                              — the جذور of the layer
Wₑ ≈ CₑVᵀ  with  Cₑ = WₑV                                — the أوزان of expert e
```

Storage per layer: independent `3·128·2048·6144 = 4.83 B` weights → factored
`3·(128·2048·r + 6144·r) = 804,864·r` weights.
- r=1024 → **5.86×** compression → 75 layers @1.58 bpw = **12.2 GB** [ARITH]
- r=512 → **11.7×** → **6.1 GB** [ARITH]

### 4.3 تثليث Tathlīth — Ternarization `T`
The triliteral move: collapse the weight alphabet to three symbols.
BitNet-b1.58 absmean, group 128: `s = mean|w|`, `q = clip(round(w/s), −1, +1)`.
Information floor log₂3 = **1.585 bpw**; Bonsai's realized rate 1.71 bpw.
**Gate G2/ARB-8 (running now):** nobody has ever tested whether *post-hoc*
ternary preserves GLM-5.2 — every residency projection assumes it. Layer-40
patch → llama.cpp judges [GATED].

### 4.4 تجويد Tajwīd — Distillation `D`
Recitation is not compressed speech — it is *retrained* speech in a stricter
form. Zero-shot factorization loses 1.3–2× (prior art); the closer is training
the factored-ternary student against the FP teacher **on the measured workload
strata** (train/holdout splits already built: `calib_workload` 31 chunks,
`calib_arabic` 29 chunks, holdouts reserved). Axolotl QLoRA fits one 5090
(~23 GiB, vaulted doctrine 2026-07-22); the coefficient blocks `Cₑ` are the
trainable surface — the basis V can stay frozen, which is *exactly* how Arabic
works: **the roots are closed-class; only the patterns productively adapt**
[GATED — needs GPU hours, G6].

## 6. Geometry and recursion — the "spatial aware, recursively learning" brain

Two more correspondences, both with real mathematical teeth:

**Spatial: the root system is a tree, and trees live in hyperbolic space.**
Each jadhr is the parent of its derived forms (8.39 children/root measured
here; unbounded in the classical lexicon). Sarkar's theorem: trees embed in
the hyperbolic plane with arbitrarily low distortion — which Euclidean space
cannot do. `poincare.py` (on disk, NEOCORTEX) is the Poincaré-ball embedding
layer: the brain's knowledge graph gets hyperbolic coordinates, so *derivation
distance* (root → form, concept → instance) is geometrically native. That is
the honest meaning of "spatial awareness" here: the memory index and concept
space are curved to match the derivational tree the language already uses.

**Recursive: iʿrāb is inference-time adaptation.**
Arabic words re-inflect *in context* — the ending changes with the sentence,
the root never does. The computational twin is test-time training (`ttt.py`):
the coefficient surfaces (أوزان) adapt on the working stream while the basis
(جذور) stays frozen. Combined with the external memory (`memory.py`) and the
sparse-activation dynamics (`hive.py`, `liquid.py`), the recursion loop is:
**recall → infer → adapt coefficients → write back to memory** — learning that
compounds across sessions without touching the roots. (The full 8-heresy wiring
is specified against the artifact record in the architecture spec that follows
this doc.)

## 8. Falsifiability ledger

- Flat G1 spectrum (rank@95% ≫ 1024 on all kinds/layers) → factorization dies
  the way pruning died, and the honest fallback is ternary QAT (Bonsai recipe)
  + 2-GPU residency + distillation to a smaller dense student.

  4-bit coeffs too: 22.8 GB uses ternary; 4-bit coeffs = 37.7 GB — over; so G2
  failing forces r ≤ 640 at 4-bit, or QAT).
- G3 fails at r=1024 → re-measure at r=1536/2048 and re-run the budget; the
  32 GB claim weakens honestly.
- Arabic-90% failing G5 on *Arabic* holdout would kill the selection leg only —
  rows C/D survive without selection entirely.
